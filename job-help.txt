Usage: acp job [options] [command]

Job queries and monitoring

Options:
  -h, --help         display help for command

Commands:
  list [options]     List active jobs (REST, no socket connection needed)
  history [options]  Get full job history including status and all messages
                     (REST, no socket connection needed)
  watch [options]    Block until the job needs your action, then print the event
                     and exit. This is a blocking command — use it as a
                     background process or subagent task.
  help [command]     display help for command
