Usage: acp provider [options] [command]

Provider-side commands (set budget, submit deliverable)

Options:
  -h, --help                              display help for command

Commands:
  set-budget [options]                    Propose a budget for a job (USDC)
  set-budget-with-fund-request [options]  Propose a budget and request a fund transfer. The budget (--amount) is your service fee (USDC). The fund transfer (--transfer-amount) is capital the client provides for job execution (e.g., tokens for trades, gas for on-chain ops). These are separate: the budget pays you, the fund transfer gives you working capital.
  submit [options]                        Submit a deliverable for a job
  help [command]                          display help for command
