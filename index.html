topoWorkbench — Static Structural (SIMP)

Quadrilateral plane-stress topology optimization in your browser: CG solver, sensitivity filter + OC update, zero dependencies. Pro result controls port ideas from rodin (C++, Heaviside projection + density filter) and zfergus/topopt (Python/MIT: penal continuation + von Mises stress).
